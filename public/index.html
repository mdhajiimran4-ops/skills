About SkillTrack

Bridging the critical gap between vocational training and sustained, long-term employment across Maharashtra's 36 districts.

Our Strategic Mission

Operating under the aegis of the Maharashtra State Skill Development Society (MSSDS).

  • Eliminate fraudulent training certificates through cryptographic serial IDs.
  • Directly verify 3, 6, and 12-month post-hiring candidate retention.
  • Align training center curriculums with live industry job vacancies.
  • Deliver automated AI skill gap alerts to state policy directors.

How It Works

A transparent 4-stage lifecycle ensuring quality, accountability, and verifiable employment.

1

Candidate Registration & Assessment

Students enroll with their educational background, GitHub, and LinkedIn profiles. Baseline competency is recorded.

2

Accredited Institute Training

Students complete state-subsidized programs, practical software deliverables, and earn signed credentials.

3

Industry AI Matching & Hiring

Employers evaluate verified candidates, match required skills, and issue formal hiring offers.

4

Quarterly Retention Follow-up

Automated check-ins at 3, 6, and 12 months track ongoing employment, wage growth, and career progression.
